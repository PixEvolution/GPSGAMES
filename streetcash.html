⚠️ REAL WORLD RULES

The Streets is played on real sidewalks, with your real body, in the real world — which famously does not have a respawn button.

Eyes up. Traffic, curbs, strangers, and lampposts are all playing on hardcore mode.

No trespassing. If a mission spot lands on private property or somewhere sketchy, place it somewhere else — that's why you choose the spots.

Never play while driving. Passenger seat or parked, always.

The cops in the game chase you. Real ones don't know you're playing. Stay out of dangerous situations — you can't finish the story from a hospital.
